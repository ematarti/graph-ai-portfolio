Executive Summary

Dear Anthony Bottles,

Company Overview: EMERGENCY NGO ETS

Founded in 1994 in Milan, Italy, EMERGENCY NGO ETS has established itself as a premier humanitarian entity. With a primary focus on victims of war, poverty, and landmines, the organization operates sophisticated healthcare facilities in some of the world's most challenging environments. As a recognized partner of the UN Department of Public Information, your commitment to "sustainable healthcare" requires robust data collection to prove impact, optimize resources, and maintain the highest standards of medical ethics.

Top GenAI Opportunities for Data Collection

Vison-to-Structured Data

Intelligent Field Record Extraction

Use multimodal GenAI (Vision-Language Models) to digitize hand-written medical charts and field notes in remote locations. This allows for immediate data structuring without manual entry, reducing human error in high-pressure medical environments.

Natural Language Processing

Multilingual Patient Intake Bots

Deploy voice-enabled GenAI agents to conduct initial patient interviews in local dialects. These agents can collect qualitative data, perform initial triage categorization, and translate findings into standardized Italian or English reports for your medical staff.

Synthetic Data & Privacy

Humanitarian Impact Analytics

Leverage GenAI to synthesize and anonymize patient data for research and grant reporting. This ensures strict adherence to GDPR and ethical standards while providing rich, structured datasets to demonstrate the efficacy of EMERGENCY’s interventions to donors.

Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1: Foundation (0-3 Months)

Conduct a data privacy audit and pilot a simple GenAI OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool to digitize historical records in a single facility.

Phase 2: Integration (3-9 Months)

Develop custom API connections between GenAI models and your existing Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems to automate data flow.

Phase 3: Scaling (12+ Months)

Roll out voice-to-data collection tools across all international missions, focusing on local language support and offline-first capabilities.

Next Steps

  • Define Key Metrics: Identify which data points are currently the most difficult to collect in the field.
  • Technical Feasibility Study: Assess the connectivity constraints in your primary geographic areas of operation.
